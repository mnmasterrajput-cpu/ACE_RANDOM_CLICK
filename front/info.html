About Us

Welcome to ACE-RandomClick, a fun and simple image-sharing platform created for students of ACE College.

ACE-RandomClick was created as a college fun project where students can share images and interact with posts through likes and comments.

The platform is designed to provide students with a simple place to share moments, participate in conversations, and enjoy a casual community experience.

What You Can Do

  • Upload images to the platform.
  • Like images shared by other users.
  • Comment on uploaded images.
  • Report content that may violate our rules.
  • Request removal of an image when appropriate.

Our Company

ACE-RandomClick is a fun project associated with Cevora.

Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. ACE-RandomClick is designed as a simple student-focused platform and does not require users to create an account or log in.

Information We Collect

ACE-RandomClick does not intentionally collect personal information such as passwords, phone numbers, home addresses, or other unnecessary personal information from users.

Comments

Comments submitted on the platform are stored in our database so that they can be displayed with the relevant image.

Uploaded Images

Images uploaded by users are stored using Cloudinary. Uploaded content may be displayed publicly on the website.

Content Removal

Users can report content that they believe violates our rules. Reported or inappropriate content may be reviewed and removed when necessary.

Advertising

We may introduce advertising services such as Google AdSense in the future. If advertising or third-party services are introduced, this privacy policy may be updated to explain their use of cookies, advertising technologies, and related data practices.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, its features, or applicable requirements.

Terms & Conditions

By using ACE-RandomClick, you agree to use the website responsibly and follow the rules described below.

Acceptable Use

Users may upload images and interact with content on the platform for lawful and appropriate purposes.

Prohibited Content

Users must not upload, share, or promote content that is illegal, sexually explicit, pornographic, abusive, hateful, threatening, violent, or otherwise inappropriate for a student-oriented platform.

Content that violates applicable laws or the rights of other people is also prohibited.

Copyright and Ownership

Users should only upload content that they have the right to share. Users should not upload copyrighted material, private material, or someone else's content without the necessary permission.

Content Removal

Users can report content that they believe violates these Terms & Conditions. We reserve the right to review and remove content that violates our rules or applicable requirements.
